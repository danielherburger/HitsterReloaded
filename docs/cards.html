Player

1 · Load songs

Log in with Spotify to load a playlist.

Spotify only lets small private apps read playlists you own or collaborate on. If it's someone else's playlist, open it in Spotify → ⋯ → Add to other playlist → New playlist, then use your copy's link.

2 · Check the songs

Spotify gives the release date of the album, so remasters and compilations often show the wrong year. Rows with a yellow year look suspicious — please double-check them. Everything here is editable and saved in this browser.

3 · Design & print

Print double-sided on thick paper (or print fronts and backs separately and glue them together), at 100% scale / “actual size”, then cut along the dashed lines. The QR codes contain only the player link plus a song ID — no title — so nobody can cheat by scanning with a normal camera app. The player link must be where you host the player (it defaults to this site).
