1 · Your playlist

Log in with Spotify to load your playlist.

Add or remove songs in Spotify whenever you like, then press Update: new songs show up here as NEW, and you can print just those. The playlist must be one you own or collaborate on. If it's someone else's, use Add to other playlist → New playlist in Spotify to make your own copy.

Other ways to add songs (CSV import)

Import a CSV, e.g. from Exportify, or one you saved with “Download backup”.

2 · Check the years

Spotify gives the release date of the album, so remasters and compilations often show the wrong year. Yellow years look suspicious, so please double-check them. You can edit everything, and it's saved automatically in this browser.

YearArtistTitle

3 · Print

Print double-sided at 100% / actual size on thick paper, then cut along the dashed lines. Can't print double-sided? Print the odd pages, put the stack back in the printer and print the even pages (or glue the two sides together).

Did the cards print OK? Mark them as printed so they won't be printed again next time.
